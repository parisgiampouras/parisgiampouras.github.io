I’m delighted that our paper, [**Align & Invert**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16870), has been accepted to **NeurIPS 2026**! We use representation alignment at inference time to guide image reconstruction with diffusion and flow-based models. Congratulations to my PhD student, **Loukas Sfountouris**, on his first PhD paper, and many thanks to **Giannis Daras** for the collaboration!
