I am an Assistant Professor of Machine Learning and AI in the [Department of Computer Science at the University of Warwick](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/), where I am a member of the Foundations of AI and Machine Learning ([FAM](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/research/fam/)) division. I am also a part-time Research Scientist at [FAIR, Meta AI](https://ai.meta.com/research/) within Meta Superintelligence Labs, and an Affiliated Researcher at the [Archimedes AI Research Center](https://archimedesai.gr/en/).

Previously, I was a Research Faculty member at the [Mathematical Institute for Data Science (MINDS) at Johns Hopkins University](https://www.minds.jhu.edu), where I held a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Global Fellowship from 2019 to 2022.

My research connects **the mathematical foundations of learning with the adaptation and alignment of modern AI models**. I combine theoretical analysis, algorithm design, and empirical evaluation to understand how models acquire useful representations, adapt efficiently to new tasks, and retain previously learned knowledge. My work on low-rank matrix factorization, tensor decomposition, and optimization complements my research on generative models, robust probabilistic inference, and continual learning.

At Meta, my work includes contributions to the post-training pipeline for **Muse Glimmer**, an open-weight language model, and research on **AI agents for automated machine-learning experimentation**. My current interests include continual and parameter-efficient adaptation of LLMs, post-training of diffusion and flow models, and agents that use reasoning, feedback, and experimentation to improve their research decisions.

Examples of my work include:

- **Representation alignment:** [Align & Invert](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16870) (NeurIPS 2026) applies alignment with pretrained visual encoders at inference time to improve image reconstruction with diffusion and flow models.
- **Causal abstraction and transportability:** [Distributionally Robust Causal Abstractions](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/felekis26a.html) (ICML 2026) and [Generalised Transportability via Causal Abstractions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15645) (2026) develop theory and algorithms for robust causal modelling and transferring causal conclusions across environments and populations.
- **Efficient foundation-model training:** [DES-LOC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22549) (ICLR 2026) develops adaptive optimizers that reduce communication costs while preserving convergence guarantees.
- **Continual learning:** [The Ideal Continual Learner](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/peng23a.html) (ICML 2023) provides a theoretical framework for learning sequential tasks without catastrophic forgetting.

**RAISE Lab** develops foundations and algorithms for **Reliable, Adaptive, and Self-Improving AI**. Our research brings together optimization and structured representations, generative-model alignment, continual adaptation, and AI research agents. [Learn more about the lab](/repsai/).

I co-organized the [ReALM–GEN workshop at ICLR 2026](https://realm-gen-workshop.github.io/), bringing together researchers working on constrained and preference-aligned diffusion and flow-based generative models. Workshop material is available on the [ICLR virtual platform](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/workshop/10000793).

I also co-organize the [Foundations of AI Seminar](https://faiseminarswarwick.github.io/) series at Warwick. Prospective speakers are welcome to [get in touch](mailto:paris.giampouras@warwick.ac.uk).

I welcome enquiries from prospective students and collaborators interested in **optimization and structured learning, generative models, continual adaptation, or AI research agents**. Please [contact me](mailto:paris.giampouras@warwick.ac.uk) to discuss potential research opportunities.
